# OSINT-Digital-Forensics-Research

With the focus on OSINT practices and Digital Forensics, this repository contains a series of reports that were written and uploaded to utilize these skills. To investigate the validity of sources and areas of concern, I am applying OSINT practices and digital forensics / cybersecurity processes to acknowledge and notify my thoughts on the matter with evidence as a basis of my analysis and opinion. 

## WARNING:
All of the reports that have been uploaded are solely for practice and educational purposes. All resources have been gathered from public sources or with the guidance of online tutorials (credits included in the reports). The analysis that were derived from the evidence is not intended to fuel any narrative or fear regarding an individual or group. All situations are intended to demonstrate a method of analyzing different cases. 

## How evidence and details were gathered:
- The evidence and analysis were gathered as objectively as possible. Using only the details that were given from the general trends that were observed during each investigation, each opinion seeks to not partake in any particular narrative or opinion.
- With each piece of evidence, I used the "When, Where, How, What, and Why" to gather information to formulate my thoughts with as little bias as possible. The "Why" question could lead to a subjective motive / reason, which is why all relevant information was meant to be noted, especially with potential motive that each piece of evidence attempts to convey (to see if there is a correlation or connection)
- Tying the evidence and the information together (ideally in a chronological order), the conclusions / overall analysis of the files and pieces of information were attempted to be constructed to the best of my ability.
- Using online resources (e.g. YouTube, TryHackMe, etc.), I sought to utilize different skills and approaches to analyzing different cases. With the cases that involve different languages, I sought to utilize translator apps to get a rough understanding of what was said into English.
- Discovered potential red flags will be noted, especially if there were details that could question the validity of the information that was discovered.
