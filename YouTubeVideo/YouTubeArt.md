## Background Information:
While watching a YouTube video, it appeared that there was a photo that appeared for a split second between minute 3 and 4 where the creator's desktop was partly revealed.

Google.com
DeviantArt.com (https://www.deviantart.com/adamscythe/journal/New-Commission-Info-1121665072)

## Evidence:
- The drawing is of a girl with glowing yellow eyes, pink-ish hair, purple-toned jacket, a cartoon shirt with a confused rat and the phrase "OH RATS!," green shoes, and a grungy atmosphere. There are also three rats that appear to be showing a lot of affection to the female character. 
- This drawing was found in the YouTube video titled, "FileTyoe: The Best Advanced Search Operator for OSINT Investigations?" (https://youtu.be/z9gr8yRSPZg?si=5Dve5D8YD0eB3f_f), which was uploaded on October 15, 2023. 
- From the Google Image Search results, the drawing is titled, "Involuntary Rat Queen" by Adam Scythe. 
- In a Google Search, I searched, "adam scythe involuntary rat queen price," which led to a search result titled, "New Commission Infro by AdamScythe on DeviantArt."
--> This appeared to be a DeviantArt page that belongs to AdamScythe, matching the name of the creator of the art piece. This drawing was found under the category "Complete Illustation A (character plus background): starts at $190 (depends on the details)." As a result, the cost for a similar piece by the artist would be around $190. 

## Analysis / Conclusion:
- From the found evidence, the desktop wallpaper was a drawing titled, "Involuntary Rat Queen" by Adam Scythe. It was created on November 25, 2022, and this information could be found on Scythe's DeviantArt commissions page that advertises artwork with prices. Based on the artist's commission information, the general price for a similar piece would be $190.
